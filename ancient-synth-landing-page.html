Features

Five thousand years in five engines

𓂀

Wavetable

8 historically-researched waveforms: gut strings, bronze bells, reed pipes, vocal chants. 8-frame morphing with 32-harmonic additive synthesis.

𓃭

FM Synthesis

4-operator FM with 8 algorithms. Inharmonic bell tones, nasal reed timbres, and metallic percussion impossible with subtractive synthesis.

𓇳

Granular

32-voice granular engine with 8 source timbres. Scatter, density, and pitch randomization create evolving textures from wind to spirit voices.

𓋴

Karplus-Strong

Physical modeling of plucked strings with body, pick position, and sustain controls. Noise burst mode for ancient frame drums.

𓊝

Waveguide

Bowed/struck string modeling with two-pole LP damping and allpass stiffness. From temple drones to metallic idiophones.

𓁹

Historical Tuning

6 tuning systems: Equal Temperament, Just Intonation, Pythagorean, Sumerian Pentatonic, Egyptian Modal, Mesopotamian Diatonic.
